How to use Offload CRM

Offload CRM keeps your customers, their contacts, projects, tasks and every call, e-mail and meeting in one place. This guide walks you through each screen. Use the contents on the left to jump to a topic.

🔑Getting started

  1. Open the app in your browser. You land on the Sign in page.
  2. Enter your e-mail and password (your administrator gives you both).
  3. Click Sign in. The Dashboard opens.
  4. On your first sign-in, change your password: account icon → Change password.
Locked out? After several wrong passwords the account is locked for a short time. Wait a few minutes, or ask an administrator to reset your password.

🗒️Activities

An activity is a record of communication: a phone call, e-mail, meeting, note or other. Link it to a customer and, optionally, to one of the customer's projects.

  1. Click + New activity on the Activities page, or Log activity on a customer or project.
  2. Choose the type, the customer (and project), the date and time.
  3. Write a short subject (optional) and the description, then Save.

🔍Search, filter and sort

All lists (customers, projects, tasks, activities, users) work the same way:

ActionHow
SearchType into the search box. It ignores upper/lower case and searches as you type.
FilterUse the drop-downs and checkboxes next to the search box.
SortClick a column header. Click again to reverse the order (▲ / ▼).
PageUse the pager under the list and pick how many rows to show per page.
Share a viewSearch, filters, sort and page are kept in the page address. Bookmark it, or send the link to a colleague.
Formats: dates are shown as dd.MM.yyyy, amounts as 1 250,50 €, and times in the Europe/Bratislava time zone.

📝Forms and dialogs

  • New and Edit open in a dialog over the page.
  • Required fields are marked. If something is wrong, the field turns red with an explanation, and nothing is saved.
  • Press Esc or click Cancel to close without saving.
  • Deleting always asks for confirmation first.
  • Websites: https:// is added automatically when missing.

👤Your account

Change password

  1. Click the account icon in the top bar → Change password.
  2. Enter your current password and the new one twice. The new password needs at least 8 characters, a digit and an uppercase letter.
  3. Save. You stay signed in here; your other sessions (other browsers and devices) are signed out.

Language

Click the language button (EN / SK) next to your name and pick a language. The page reloads in that language.

⚙️Settings

Everyone can open Settings, but most changes are for administrators.

TabWhat it doesWho
Project statusesAdd, rename, recolor and reorder statuses (Move up / Move down). Mark a status as Counts as active or Closed, and pick the ☆ default status for new projects. The default must be active and not closed. A status used by projects can't be deleted.Admin edits, everyone sees
GeneralVersion, environment, culture, currency and time zone.Everyone
DatabaseSwitch the whole app between the Production and the Test database. All users are switched and all open tabs reload.Admin
UsersCreate users, edit names and roles, reset passwords, deactivate / reactivate users. Users are never deleted; a deactivated user can't sign in, and open sessions end within about a minute.Admin
Database switch: while the orange TEST DATABASE badge is shown, you are working with test data. Switch back to Production before doing real work.

Roles

User
Works with customers, contacts, projects, tasks and activities, and can change their own password and language.
Administrator
Everything a User can do, plus users, project statuses and the database switch.
